Two tokens.
Different roles.

$CUM — the existing project token

$CUM remains part of the project's direction. Specific companion integrations are still being defined; holding it does not currently grant companion access or AI usage credit.

xCUM — planned AI service credits

We're building xCUM as prepaid credit for cumAI and companion services. The planned flow lets users buy, transfer or gift xCUM, then activate it into a shared AI usage balance.

The xCUM purchase and activation system is in development. xCUM is separate from $CUM.

How would xCUM purchase and activation work?

In the planned flow, USDG purchases xCUM; ETH is converted to USDG first. You could activate xCUM immediately into AI usage credit, or keep it in your wallet to transfer, gift or activate later.

Activation burns the xCUM and adds usage credit to the chosen AI account. Activation is one-way: the resulting AI balance cannot be converted back into xCUM. This system is still in development.
