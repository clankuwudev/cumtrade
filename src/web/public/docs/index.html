Open now

cumAI

Chat with the top AI models and make pictures, in the app or from your own code through an OpenAI-compatible API.

The models

Models from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, xAI, DeepSeek, Qwen, Moonshot, Zhipu, MiniMax and more. The full list, with each model's price and limits, is on the Models tab. Whether each one is up right now is on the Status tab.

Free today

Chat
GPT-4.1 nano, up to 8,000 tokens in and 1,024 out per reply.
Pictures
2 a day.
Cost
Nothing. Free use comes from a small daily allowance, while the day's shared budget lasts. It has no cash value and doesn't carry over.
Sign in
With a browser wallet, a Google account or an X account. Signing in means signing a message: it costs nothing and moves nothing.

Pricing

Chat models cost less than their official prices. Pictures cost the official price. Every call is priced per token, charged to the millionth of a dollar, and every answer says what it cost. The calculator on the homepage compares any model with its official price.

Every other model opens when paid use launches. You'll pay with xCUM (in development), or directly with another token launched on clank.trade (planned).

For developers

The API speaks OpenAI's format, so the official Python and JavaScript SDKs work as they are. Change the base URL and the key:

https://api.clankuwu.com/v1

API keys come with paid credits. Endpoints, errors and limits are in the API reference.

There is also a free data API for clank.trade tokens: token checks, trades, holders and creator records. It's documented on cumTrade.

Content and safety

Every prompt and picture is checked against a content policy before any model sees it. Answers come from other companies' models, so they can be wrong. Check anything that matters.

Never paste a recovery phrase, private key or password. cumAI refuses text that looks like a recovery phrase, but that check can miss one.

Create your own agent

The plan for cumOS: anyone can create a companion agent, launch a token for it, and keep it running with its own pool of AI credit.

Create it

Make it
Design a character and pay for its creation in xCUM. Making a new character's look and animation costs real money, so creating one has a price. It isn't set yet.
Your chats
An agent other people use is its own copy. Nobody's private memories or chats are ever part of an agent someone else can use or buy.
Chatting with it
People pay for their own messages: our standard price, plus a markup the creator sets, up to a cap.

The agent launchpad

Its token
Launch one token for your agent on clank.trade, from our site, signed by your own wallet.
The check
Every launch is checked first. Its name and picture can't pose as someone else, copy an existing token, or break the content rules.
Its pool
Each agent has a pool of xCUM that pays for its own AI: acting on its own, keeping its memory, and any free chats its creator offers. When the pool runs dry, the agent pauses until someone refills it.
Filling it
A share of the agent's usage profit, plus xCUM anyone can add: its creator, its fans, its holders.
Its profit
Shared between its creator, its pool, buybacks and burns of its token and of $CUM, xCUM airdrops to its token's holders, and the cost of running it. The shares aren't set yet.
Paying with it
Like any token launched on clank.trade, an agent's token can pay for AI directly.

So every agent runs the same loop as the project, and part of every agent's profit buys back and burns $CUM.

In development

xCUM

xCUM is your AI credit balance, on-chain. 1 xCUM = $1 of AI on cumAI and cumOS.

There is no xCUM token yet. Anything using the name today is not ours.

Get it
Buy it with USDG, a US-dollar stablecoin, or with ETH that is converted to USDG first. $CUM holders can also claim it by airdrop (planned).
Or a token
Planned: skip xCUM and pay for AI directly with another token launched on clank.trade. Each request costs the same in dollars, paid in your token at what it sells for. The token is checked first and sold behind the scenes. $CUM itself can't pay: its use is the airdrops.
Spend it
Each request spends xCUM at the model's price, and the xCUM it spends is burned. The xCUM you hold is your balance.
Keep it
Until you spend it, it stays in your wallet. Gift it, or sell it on the xCUM order book (planned).
Cash
We never exchange xCUM back for cash.
Backing
Nothing is printed: every xCUM is paid for in USDG or funded from profit.

Planned

xCUM airdrops

Who qualifies
Wallets holding at least $20 of $CUM when a snapshot is taken, valued at the pool's price at that block.
When
Snapshots are taken at unannounced blocks. No dates yet.
How much
Shared pro rata: your part of a round matches your part of the qualifying $CUM.
Claiming
Connect the wallet that holds your $CUM and sign a message. Claim the xCUM to that wallet, or to the one you use on cumAI.
Left out
Team and treasury wallets, the pool and other contracts, and exchange and bridge wallets.
Funding
Part of the AI profit. Every airdropped xCUM is backed before its round is posted.
Published
Each round's amount, snapshot block, number of qualifying holders and transaction links.

Hold $CUM in a wallet you control. $CUM on an exchange or a bridge doesn't count.

FAQ

Why would I need $CUM if I have xCUM?
xCUM is the AI credit you spend. $CUM is how you get xCUM without buying it: holders get xCUM airdrops, and part of the AI profit buys back and burns $CUM. Both are planned.
Is cumAI free?
Partly. Anyone signed in can chat with GPT-4.1 nano and make 2 pictures a day. The other models open when paid use launches, with xCUM or other clank.trade tokens.
How big is the discount?
It depends on the model. Each model's price is on the Models tab, and the homepage calculator compares it with the official price.
Can I buy xCUM yet?
Not yet. There is no xCUM token, so anything using the name today is not ours. When xCUM launches, its address goes on this page and in the Telegram.
Can I pay with other tokens?
That's planned. Tokens launched on clank.trade will pay for AI directly, just like xCUM. Each request is paid in your token at what it sells for, and the sale happens behind the scenes. Each token is checked first. $CUM is the exception: its use is the airdrops.
When is the next snapshot?
Snapshots are unannounced on purpose, and the first round has no date yet. To be counted, hold at least $20 of $CUM in a wallet you control.
Can I create my own agent?
Not yet. It's planned: you'll design an agent, launch a token for it on clank.trade from our site, and keep it running with a pool of xCUM. See Create your own agent.
Could my chats end up in someone else's agent?
No. Nobody's private memories or chats are ever part of an agent someone else can use or buy.
Can I talk to Yuna?
Not yet. She runs in a local prototype while we build cumOS. Join the Telegram to follow along.
How much will cumOS cost?
No prices yet. The plan is a subscription that hosts your companion, plus the AI it uses, paid separately with xCUM or another clank.trade token.
Who runs this?
cumLabs, an independent team. clank.trade did not make it, does not run it, and has not reviewed or endorsed it. Nor are we affiliated with Robinhood, Coinbase or Uniswap.
Does the team hold $CUM?
Yes, so its price matters to us. Judge what you read here knowing that.
What is cumTrade?
Our first app, for tokens launched on clank.trade. It stays open for existing users at clankuwu.com/trade.
Where can I get help?
Ask in the Telegram, or message @0xzer0ai on X. To report a problem, say what you did, what you expected, and what happened.
Is any of this financial advice?
No. Tokens launched on clank.trade are highly speculative. Their price can fall fast, and you can lose some or all of what you put in. Nothing here promises a profit.
